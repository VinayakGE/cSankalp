# cSankalp – A Hybrid Finance Ecosystem

## Executive Summary

**Mission & Vision**  
cSankalp bridges traditional finance and decentralized systems by offering a regulated, inclusive Hybrid Finance (HyFi) platform. Our mission is to empower individuals, merchants, and institutions with a secure, sovereign financial infrastructure built for the digital era.

## What is Hybrid Finance (HyFi)?

Hybrid Finance (HyFi) integrates the benefits of traditional financial systems (TradFi) with decentralized technologies (DeFi), ensuring:
- **Regulatory alignment**
- **Blockchain-level transparency**
- **Cross-border financial inclusion**
- **Real-world utility with digital efficiency**

HyFi is the necessary bridge to make digital assets legally viable and economically sustainable across emerging and established markets.

## Ecosystem Overview

**Core Principles**
- Dual-token architecture (public components)
- Modular compliance & transparency
- Financial inclusion at scale
- Sovereignty-first infrastructure

**Key Components**
- **c₹ (Crypto Rupee):** Utility token used within the cSankalp ecosystem for seamless digital payments and cross-border transactions.
- **Regulatory Blockchain Layer:** Ensures all transactions follow compliance protocols (aligned with India-first legal frameworks).
- **Polygon Blockchain:** Powers scalable cross-border functionalities.

---

## Key Features (Public Scope)

### 1. Token Utility: c₹ (Crypto Rupee)
- Serves as a medium for domestic & international digital transactions.
- Supports merchant payments, remittances, and decentralized finance use cases.
- Enables programmable money logic and smart contract flows.

### 2. Onboarding Flow (Simplified for Public View)
- INR > e₹ (CBDC or compliant fiat-backed digital representation) > c₹
- KYC/AML integrated at onboarding level (zk-compliance in private scope)
- Seamless fiat-to-digital asset bridging

### 3. Merchant & Fintech API Integration
- Enable fintechs and merchants to accept c₹
- Compliant digital ledger for audits and reconciliation
- Real-time transaction status and low-fee settlement rails
